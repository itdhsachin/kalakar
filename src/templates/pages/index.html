Welcome to the Kala Guru by Ira Rangoli Arts

KalaGuru is a unique platform dedicated to providing structured and professional art training at an incredibly affordable cost. But it’s not just about learning—the platform also hosts engaging mini-competitions designed to help artists refine their skills and present their creations in the best possible way. Our goal is to nurture talent, encourage creativity, and provide a space where artists can grow and shine.

Our Mission

Empowering artists through structured training and fostering creativity.

1
Providing Professional Art Training – We aim to offer structured and expert-led art education to everyone at an affordable cost.
2
Spreading Art Awareness – Promoting the importance and beauty of art across communities.
3
Reaching Every Corner – From small villages to big cities, making art education accessible to all.
4
Encouraging Participation – Motivating individuals to actively engage in artistic endeavors and showcase their talents.
5
Building a Creative Community – Connecting artists and enthusiasts through the love of art.

📢 Announcement

Hello! In the first year of our KalaGuru initiative, we chose Rangoli Art as the first artistic form to promote. The district-level competition was conducted both online and offline in Pune.

You can now view the winners of the district-level competition on our official KalaGuru Facebook page.

  • ✅ Districts with only one submission have their artist declared the winner directly.
  • ✅ In districts with more than 3 entries, shortlisted submissions will be posted for public voting.
  • 🏆 The entry with the most likes will be the winner from that district.

This process began on 6th April 2026, with 21 days of free training offered until 26th April 2026.

Only the artists who:

  • 🎨 Uploaded their artwork after training
  • 🎥 Watched more than 50% of training videos
  • 💰 Paid the ₹500 registration fee

will be featured in the final artwork list. This initiative received tremendous response with many participants enrolling and receiving free training.

Stay tuned for more creative competitions! Follow us on Facebook, YouTube, and Instagram to stay updated.
