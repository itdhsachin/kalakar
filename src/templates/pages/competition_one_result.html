KalaGuru Rangoli Competition 2025

Organized by Ira Rangoli Arts | Pune | 1st June 2025

About the Competition

This was the first Rangoli competition organized by KalaGuru by Ira Rangoli Arts in Pune at Ira Rangoli Arts Center, Vidya Vikas Vidyalaya, Sahakar Nagar, Pune 09. The event took place on 1st June 2025 and witnessed enthusiastic participation from talented artists across Maharashtra.

All participants were the first-round winners from their respective districts. The initial round was held online following the completion of a Basic Rangoli class. The final round was conducted physically and evaluated by a jury based on creativity, technique, and presentation.

Top 3 Winners

1st: Gourav Waghmare (Amravati)

Prize: ₹35,000 + Trophy

2nd: Priti Dhanbhadur (Akola)

Prize: ₹26,000 + Trophy

3rd: Pradnya Shelke (Latur)

Prize: ₹17,000 + Trophy
